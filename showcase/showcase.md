# Carom

Two paddles. Two rotating obstacles. One very changeable line of flight. Carom is a quick, top-down duel where keeping the ball alive is only the beginning: the real game is finding an angle your opponent cannot cover.

The opening serve gives you a moment to settle in. Meet it squarely for a clean return, or catch it near the tip of your paddle to send it across the court on a sharper line. Every paddle hit adds pace, turning a measured exchange into a fast contest of positioning.

Move through the hit to put spin on the ball. A return curves away from its original line, its fading trail tracing the bend. The violet bars sway and rotate through the middle of the court; bank off a tilted face and a safe-looking shot can become an awkward scramble.

Play Solo against the machine, or share a keyboard for a local Versus match. First to eleven wins, but you need a two-point lead. At deuce, every return matters—and there is always another angle.
